Where the grid is allowed — and where it isn't

  • Hero and How-it-works section backgrounds
  • Step numbers (number box)
  • Chinese display and H2 headlines, one character per box
  • The cover and sticker-sized decorations
  • Behind body text, FAQ answers, forms or addresses — these sit on white paper cards
  • English headlines (no boxes; letters don't fit a square)
  • Long H1/H3 Chinese headlines (too many boxes to read)
  • More than two grid sections in a row
