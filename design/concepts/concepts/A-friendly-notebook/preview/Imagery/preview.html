Rule

One real photo on the homepage: Dr. Woo with a child patient. Everything else is words. No illustrations, no stock, no icons-as-pictures.

✅ Do

  • ✅Natural window light, warm and a little soft
  • ✅Dr. Woo at the child's eye level, both relaxed
  • ✅Crop 4:5, radius-lg, no filter
  • ✅Get written consent from the parent

❗ Don't

  • ❗Stock photos or AI-generated people
  • ❗Close-ups of lenses on fingertips or eyes being touched
  • ❗Illustrations, cartoons, gradients, overlays
  • ❗Text baked into the photo
