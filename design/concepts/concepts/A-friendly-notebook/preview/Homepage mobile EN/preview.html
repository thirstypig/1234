👀 Ortho-k for children, ages 7–18

Clear vision all day.
No glasses at school.

Your child wears special lenses while they sleep. In the morning, they take them out and see clearly. Ortho-k may also help slow down nearsightedness as they grow.

Is your child's prescription going up every year?

Nearsightedness often gets worse during the school years. Stronger glasses fix the blur, but they don't slow the change.

How ortho-k works

  1. Step 1👀

    Full eye exam and a map of the eye

  2. Step 2📏

    Custom lenses made for your child

  3. Step 3🌙

    Wear at night. See clearly all day.
